**QUOLL-1. Aim the language at a flat cost of change with the least reading over whole workflows, to be checked on comparable series of changes.**
*Stated by* [goal] and [measure].
*Because* the yardstick of a language for systems that keep changing is a rate: guidance on quality lowers erosion's starting level and leaves its rate unchanged, as SlopCodeBench finds, so a [goal] judged on one generation cannot tell the language from a better template, while its cost across a series of changes can.
*Because* reading belongs in the [goal] as its constraint: least reading alone is best met by reading nothing, as vibe-coding does, and a flat cost alone by reading everything, so the pair names the trade the language makes.
*Accepting* that nothing has shown the [goal] met yet and the [measure]'s protocol is neither designed nor run; detailed comparison methods and thresholds belong to the separate benchmarking project.
*Rejected* locality as the [goal], a means rather than the outcome; [goal]s ranked equal, a flat cost, least reading, one text for people and agents, simplicity, any [model.kernel.target] and regeneration among them, a list that settles no trade-off; external contracts, preserved decisions and architectural coherence as coordinate [goal]s, candidate means and evidence rather than outcomes; least reading as a byte count or as reading less than a sound change requires; a Quoll-generation-only [measure], which excludes maintained native and mixed work; a [goal] with no [measure], which no series of changes could check; and formalising requirements as such, proof and verification, mechanical semantics, a [model.kernel.target] flowing back into its source, and the quality of a single generation, each outside what the [goal] asks.

**QUOLL-2. Set the language's [purpose] at maintained source across permanent non-code contracts, canonical native code with decisions, canonical Quoll and mixed sources, derived [model.kernel.target]s and temporary design views, and its [reach] at any system designed over a long series of changes, software first, the core naming no domain.**
*Stated by* [purpose] and [reach].
*Because* a maintained abstract or non-code contract need generate nothing, canonical native code with its decisions can be [model.kernel.source], and a derived [model.kernel.target] or temporary design view has a different authority again; one universal source-to-target flow cannot state these uses.
*Because* authorship and authority are orthogonal: [model.kernel.generation activity] can change canonical or derived units, while each maintained fact retains one [model.kernel.source] and no permanent implementation mirror or lossless round trip is required.
*Because* the language's first sources are not all software: a process of design whose derived artifacts are agents' skills is among them, which a [reach] of software alone holds only by stretching the word without limit.
*Because* work beyond code, talks and decks among it, is already designed with logs of decisions, so a [reach] beyond code is practice rather than speculation.
*Because* a core naming no domain costs nothing a framework would not say anyway, kinds of contract and tests among it, so a domain's notions, software's too, come in a framework of their own.
*Because* the [goal] pays, and the [measure] has something to measure, over a long series of changes, while a design that barely changes gains little from the structure, though nothing keeps it from it.
*Accepting* a broader root notion of source and purpose, explicit authority choices in profiles and project conventions, and no guarantee that a temporary design view is faithful or complete; that the core says less in its own terms, so software's notions are written as a real framework rather than leaned on as built-in words; and that the [reach] is a hypothesis, tested so far on software and on a process of design alone.
*Rejected* maintained Quoll source alone, which excludes target-free contracts and canonical native code; a synchronized two-way mirror, which duplicates implementation authority and requires lossless round-tripping no driver needs; software with the processes that make and run it, a line that only excludes, the core gaining nothing by it; and any designed artifact with no series of changes, one-shot designs as a talk, a contract or a plan among them, over which the [goal] has nothing to measure and plain design serves well enough.

**QUOLL-3. Have agents write the language, under people's supervision or on their own, and make it for reading, by people above all, engineers and architects fluent in the design of modules who build software with AI, and by agents.**
*Stated by* [writers] and [readers].
*Because* nobody writes a source by hand: agents write it, people supervising them or not, so what the language must serve in people is reading, while a person may still write where they wish.
*Because* the language's value is an architecture of the source, which only a reader fluent in the design of modules can judge, so it assumes that design and teaches none of it.
*Because* agents and people then read the same text under the same rules, so the language keeps no dialect for either.
*Accepting* that the language speaks to those who can understand it alone, naming nobody else, since others may find it useful with an agent's help, and who will not is no list it closes.
*Rejected* people as its expected [writers], with a split between those who write and those who decide without writing, which rested on people writing by hand; a language shaped for agents first, people reading reports alone, while people read the interfaces and decisions themselves; and the language teaching the design of modules to [readers] who lack it, a methodology's work, a structure diluted for teaching losing what the language adds.

**QUOLL-4. Take modules as the language's [paradigm], every other a style a framework declares over them, in a programming language one level up that looks simple, its statements in natural language and [formal] to an agent.**
*Stated by* [paradigm], [character] and [formal].
*Because* the bound a module sets on a change is the [goal] as structure, and a module is the closed input an agent works from, as [principles.session fit] has it.
*Because* the [readers] already think in modules, their interfaces and what they depend on.
*Because* every other [paradigm] reduces to a style over modules: a concept is a module that [uses] no other's interfaces, a synchronization one that [uses] several concepts' interfaces, a connector one whose interface is the interaction, and a filter one whose interface is its input and output; so the core names no style, and each is there where it fits.
*Because* the [readers] build software with AI, so the language reads as programming one level up, higher than the languages they write in yet built as properly.
*Because* a statement an agent can follow, and a rule one it can check, is as [formal] as an agent needs, while natural language keeps it what people read, with no mechanical semantics, which lies outside the [goal].
*Accepting* that behaviour spanning several modules, as one feature's flow through them, has no unit of its own, living in the [model.kernel.body] of the module containing them or in a module of its own that [uses] their interfaces; and that a style is only as strong as the [model.profile.framework rules] declaring it and the keeping of them.
*Rejected* objects, a module extending another and inheriting its items, under which a change to an ancestor reaches every descendant and an agent reads the whole chain to know what one says, while implementing interfaces gives subtyping anyway; concepts and synchronizations as the core, which forbid units to depend on one another, need a [formal] vocabulary of actions, mechanical semantics outside the [goal], and suit interactive applications rather than any system; dataflow as the core, since most systems are not flows; and a graph of fine-grained typed items joined by typed links, with no modules, under which nothing bounds a [model.kernel.reference], so nothing bounds a change.

**QUOLL-5. Divide the definition into five parts, each changing for a reason of its own and kept apart by what each [uses]: [layout], [model], [grammar], [source] and [principles].**
*Stated by* [layout alone], [model alone], [grammar on layout], [source on three] and [principles on model].
*Because* how files lie on disk, how a file's text is written, what a package means, which writing states which thing of it, and what every package keeps each change for a reason of their own: storage, the form of writing, the language's terms, how those meet, and review's practice, so that one reason to change moves one part.
*Because* the first reader of the language built, a program, drew [layout], [grammar] and [model] apart in practice, each changing apart, while what [principles] holds is read by [writers] and reviewers rather than by such a program.
*Because* the parts hold interfaces and decisions alone, so no item of one is hidden from another, and what keeps each reason to change in one part is what each [uses], [model] resting on no other part.
*Because* a change of storage, as a format of the language's own in place of Markdown, then moves [layout], [grammar] and [source] and leaves [model] and [principles] as they stand, the parts most read being the least changed.
*Because* the [layout.modules] then lists each part after those it [uses], so a reader of the parts in order meets none before what it rests on.
*Because* no part has a [model.kernel.body], nor does the root implement a part's interface, so the [model.kernel.default interface]s of [layout], [grammar], [source] and [principles], and [model.kernel], [model.graph], [model.rich], [model.profile] and [model.generation], are for whatever reads the language, a toolchain first, to implement.
*Accepting* parts with no [model.kernel.body].
*Rejected* the blocks of the summary the design began from as parts, a map whose blocks mix reasons to change and hold what is not the language's; one module holding every interface, many secrets in one; and, stated in the definition, how a system is generated, who writes a package in which [model.graph.role]s, the harness agents work in, how tools are installed and how the language's site is made, each changing with practice or tooling rather than with the language, and a doctrine's, a toolchain's or a workspace's to state.

**QUOLL-6. Call [source] the part that joins the terms of [model.kernel], [model.graph], [model.rich], [model.profile] and [model.generation] to the files they are written in.**
*Stated by* [source on three].
*Because* it names the side [model.kernel], [model.graph], [model.rich], [model.profile] and [model.generation] meet, the files and what is wrong in them, and pairs with [layout.source file].
*Because* it reads as a qualifier of the terms of [model.kernel], [model.graph], [model.rich], [model.profile] and [model.generation], as [source.interface] does.
*Because* it is short, and is no word another part or a [model.generation.assembly] already gives another thing.
*Accepting* that the word also names what is maintained, now stated directly by [model.kernel.source] and [model.kernel.target] rather than left to workspace and package.
*Rejected* `encoding`, read as a character encoding, its [source.problem]s as charset errors; `representation`, the longest word in the package; `storage`, which suggests persistence and databases; `mapping`, which every part does; `binding`, a word [model.generation.assembly] uses for another thing; `reading` and `resolver`, a tool's process rather than what the part states; and `projection`, a word the language's reasons give another thing.

**QUOLL-12. Declare explicit root exposures naming the root's own [model.kernel.default interface] as `default`, [layout.default] as `layout`, [grammar.default] as `grammar`, [source.default] as `source`, [principles.default] as `principles`, and [model.kernel], [model.graph], [model.rich], [model.profile] and [model.generation] at their natural paths; publish no aggregate model interface, module or direct item alias.**
*Stated by* [own surface].
*Because* root-reachable exposures give dependents the public [language.default] interface and its items, such as [language.default.purpose], together with the part names and five natural model paths deliberately, while canonical internal paths remain private by default and no interface is public merely by existing.
*Because* every reference in an exposed contract or supplied declaration unit must land on an item of a root-reachable exposed interface or a dependency's [source.public surface], so the root default's public items and declaration references require its explicit whole-interface exposure rather than a same-unit or declaration-only exception.
*Because* an exposure aliases an existing whole interface without copying it or creating a header, module or item, so no named facade competes with a same-named module; inclusion remains composition alone and publishes no path.
*Because* local structural references and package-qualified public paths are distinct complete references: a package name is never a removable prefix, every public path has one target for internal and external readers, and ambiguity fails rather than module or facade precedence selecting a target.
*Accepting* explicit maintenance of the exposed paths, migration of the language's own references, and removal of the flat compatibility surface no consumer needs.
*Rejected* public named root facades and direct inclusion as publication, with facade or module-first precedence, which recreate ambiguous paths and aggregate coupling; exposing internal modules or individual items directly, which creates a second surface model and makes private structure public by default; leaving the root default unexposed while its items and declarations are public, which requires a same-unit or declaration-only exception; one aggregate model interface or direct item aliases such as `model.package`, which defeat selective consumption; and restating each part's items at the root, which creates two copies to drift.

**QUOLL-10. Preserve lightweight [pilot evidence] from canonical native, Quoll-owned generation and mixed source workflows, and assign rigorous comparison to a separate benchmarking project.**
*Stated by* [pilot evidence].
*Because* concrete cases and failures from each workflow inform decisions about its mechanisms and later design without turning delivery into a research program.
*Accepting* that detailed evidence plans belong to work items and the separate benchmarking project, and that the evidence informs later decisions rather than proving its mechanisms sufficient.
*Rejected* a comprehensive research matrix in every delivery; and no evidence obligation, which loses the cases and failures later design and benchmarking need.

**QUOLL-11. Prioritize model-aware [structural tooling] over items, relations, dependents and decisions while preserving canonical Markdown/TOML now, and leave concrete tools and any programming-language-like syntax to separate evidence-informed design.**
*Stated by* [structural tooling].
*Because* the present reading deficit is understanding structure and change rather than hand-editing convenience, so rendering, IDE navigation and structural diffs should expose the model people review rather than only colorized text lines.
*Because* this direction serves human reading across representations without becoming another coordinate [goal], while a concrete-syntax migration needs evidence from native and document profiles before its form or cost can be judged.
*Accepting* canonical Markdown/TOML as the bootstrap representation now, no renderer, IDE or structural-diff implementation or concrete form here, and no delivery date, plug-in API, diff format or scheduled syntax migration.
*Revisit* the programming-language-like syntax hypothesis in a dedicated concrete-syntax design pass informed by native and document profiles.
*Rejected* replacing canonical syntax together with the native-model change; plain text rendering and line diffs indefinitely, which wastes the model's structural information; and placing this language-wide direction in one representation profile or the canonical grammar.
